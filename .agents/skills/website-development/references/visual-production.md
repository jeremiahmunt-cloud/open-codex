# Visual production and rendering

Classify the visual as a real subject, proposed change, or fictional concept. Keep this distinction in its asset record and in a public caption where it matters.

## Workflow

1. Inspect reference images before editing. Identify the target and what must remain unchanged.
2. Specify subject, use, aspect ratio, crop, composition, materials, light, palette, and exclusions. Use the image-generation skill/tool for raster generation or edits.
3. For real-property edits, preserve identity, camera position, massing, and unaffected features. Added pools, structures, or finishes are proposals, not evidence of existing conditions.
4. Inspect geometry, repeated windows, reflections, stairs, rails, materials, and mobile crops. Iterate on concrete defects.
5. Put website assets in the project's durable asset pipeline with stable filenames. Create responsive derivatives with appropriate image tools, preserve the original, and document its source.
6. Add accurate alt text and adjacent concept labels. A filename or internal provenance record alone is not a public disclosure.

Use code, vector tools, or verified supplied assets for exact floor plans, maps, charts, pricing tables, precise logos, and functional UI. Keep essential text outside raster images.

## Reusable luxury concept prompt

Use case: architectural concept visualization.
Asset type: wide website hero, fictional residence.
Primary request: a fictional desert courtyard residence with a quiet European-inspired material palette, warm limestone, charcoal metalwork, tall cypress forms, and a still reflecting pool.
Composition: eye-level architectural view, restrained wide lens, architecture on the right, darker uncluttered space on the left for later HTML copy; compose for wide and narrow crops.
Lighting: blue-hour exterior with soft warm interior light, plausible shadows and reflections.
Style: refined architectural visualization, realistic materials, no extreme HDR.
Constraints: no people, signage, address, watermark, prices, measurements, or text; do not claim to document a specific property.
Publication caption: "Architectural concept — fictional residence."

Generated outputs vary. This package does not depend on a particular image or paid API. For non-property work, replace the subject and visual language with the approved brief.

## Sketches and presentations

Use a user sketch as an input when requested; it is not a prerequisite. For a requested property deck, reuse verified content and chosen images, then apply the presentation skill's editable-layout and render-review workflow. A site screenshot is not an editable slide deck.
