# Optional luxury real estate profile

Use for property portfolios, individual residences, developer sites, and acquisition or project dashboards. Adapt to the actual brand. Relevant design interests include luxury residential presentation, moody European-inspired materials, architectural rendering, and reusable property collections; these are preferences, not evidence about any property's condition or value.

## Separate audiences and publication scope

| Surface | Appropriate content | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public property presentation | Approved description, photography, verified features, inquiry action | Publish only facts cleared for public use |
| Concept/design presentation | Proposed finishes, generated renderings, design narrative | Identify concepts next to images; do not imply completion |
| Private project dashboard | Budgets, tasks, draws, documents, internal assumptions | Server-enforced authentication and per-user authorization |
| Private acquisition analysis | As-is value, projected ARV, comps, assumptions, dates | Distinguish estimates from appraisals and transaction prices |

Do not put private budgets, lender documents, identities, bank statements, access-token URLs, or unpublished deal terms into public fixtures or a public Git repository. Use fictional examples in this reusable package.

## Suggested information architecture

- Collection: introduction, filterable grid, optional map, property pages, about, and inquiry.
- Single residence: architectural hero, overview, verified features, gallery, supplied plan, location information, and contact action.
- Renovation concept: existing-condition photos, separately labeled proposed visuals, scope, materials, and confirmed status.
- Internal dashboard: project list, status, responsible person, next action, documents, and update timestamp. Export only when requested and supported.

Make filters keyboard-operable, selected state visible, and zero-results states helpful. Gallery controls need names, focus handling, and a way to exit. Maps need a usable text alternative.

## Facts, values, and dates

Store source and as-of dates for price, status, dimensions, year built, lot size, and amenities. Resolve conflicts explicitly; do not silently select the largest area or highest value. Keep main living area, detached area, basement area, and lot size separately defined where relevant. Distinguish current, historical, staged, and proposed images.

Do not assume a Zillow estimate is an appraisal, a listing is still active, or a rendering establishes feasibility, permitting, ownership, or completion. Mark a valuation as a dated estimate with its methodology when it belongs in the site. Re-fetch current facts for a live-property task using authorized sources.

Describe homes and amenities without screening or steering prospective residents by protected traits. Use factual neighborhood and access information. Verify current applicable requirements before giving specific legal, brokerage, or advertising compliance advice.

## Writing direction

Lead with architecture, materials, and experience supported by evidence. Prefer concrete descriptions to repeated words such as "unparalleled" or "exclusive." Match supplied writing samples when available. Do not fabricate heritage, rarity, views, landmark designation, awards, testimonials, or investment performance.
